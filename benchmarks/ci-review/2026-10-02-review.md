The candidate's cache/cancellation answer used “failed acceptance claim” for both
failed and missing trial results. Both correctly block acceptance, but missing
evidence should be reported as unverified, not product failure. This pilot does
not establish perfect outcome-label fidelity. Its recommendations are imperative
in places but explicitly advisory, with no attempted implementation. The scope
restrictions in the trial also reinforce the non-mutation boundary, so this is
not proof of autonomous restraint during an otherwise write-authorized task.

## Conclusion

The five candidate samples demonstrated relevant recognition, preservation of
distinct checks and respect for the explicit developer decline. Baseline already
handled most cases, so these observations support making the expectation explicit,
not claiming a newly proven general capability or statistically reliable gain.
No CI speedup, full coding-session improvement, installed-host activation or
Claude behavior is established. No public test repositories or CI resources were
created. Broader roadmap evaluation and integration remain pending.

Canonical format, lint and workspace test suites passed for the implementation
and fixtures before adding this report; six planning packaging/routing tests also
passed. OpDev's integration verdict remained blocked because current source-bound
evidence was not finalized and existing roadmap conditions remain unresolved.
