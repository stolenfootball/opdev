## Limits and disposition

Local validation after the final guidance: canonical formatting and Clippy passed;
workspace tests initially failed two existing npm-shim tests inside the restricted
sandbox, then passed in full when rerun with host access. Four environment-gated
tests remain ignored (two Docker, two remote-provider trials); the separate timeout
helper is invoked by its parent test. The Codex plugin validator passed. The skill
validator initially could not read its task-local PyYAML dependency in the sandbox,
then passed with host access. These retries are tooling/environment observations,
not evidence that the initial attempts passed. No integration CI or post-merge
qualification has been performed for this uncommitted candidate.

Automatic skill routing, actual fresh-context authority retrieval, real tracker
access, missing-authority variant, unrestricted tool behavior and marketplace
installation remain unverified. These are fresh sessions with supplied context,
not end-to-end plugin adoption trials. Small, adaptively corrected trials do not
estimate a general success rate or demonstrate a model-independent improvement.

Keep the feature advisory and on demand. The skill-creation workflow influenced
validation by separating packaged-link checks from semantic host behavior; passing
the former does not erase the latter's failures. The implementation has no new
CLI/schema, mandatory project document, automatic issue creation or gate authority.
Human wording review and a decision about the remaining host limitations are
pending. Do not close #46 or claim integration/release completion from this record.
